What is fancy text?

Fancy text is ordinary text written with special Unicode characters that look like bold, cursive, gothic or bubble fonts. Because it is still plain text — not an image or an installed font — it works in apps that never let you choose a font. Type in the box above, tap any style to copy it, then paste it into a bio, username, caption or chat.

Where to use fancy text

  • Social bios & names: a script style like 𝓨𝓸𝓾𝓻 𝓝𝓪𝓶𝓮 makes an Instagram or TikTok profile instantly recognisable.
  • Gaming & Discord nicknames: gothic and framed styles such as 𝔜𝔬𝔲𝔯 𝔑𝔞𝔪𝔢 stand out in lobbies and server lists.
  • Captions & comments: a bold style like 𝐘𝐨𝐮𝐫 𝐍𝐚𝐦𝐞 adds emphasis where platforms offer no formatting buttons.
  • Chats & statuses: small caps like ʏᴏᴜʀ ɴᴀᴍᴇ keep WhatsApp and Telegram statuses tidy but distinctive.

Tips for best results

  • Instagram & TikTok bios: Keep it short (under 30 characters) so it doesn't get cut off. Bold and small-caps styles are the most readable.
  • Colors: Pick any color on each card — the color is copied together with your text as rich text, so pasting into Google Docs, Microsoft Word, Notion or an email keeps the color. Instagram, TikTok and Discord bios only accept plain text, so there the fancy characters apply but color can't travel with them.
  • Accessibility: Screen readers may read fancy Unicode letter-by-letter. For important names or business profiles, use fancy text for decoration only — not for your main name.
  • Favourites: Tap the ★ on any card to pin it to the top, so your go-to styles are always first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this fancy text generator free?

Yes, completely free. No account, no download, no watermark. Type, copy and paste as many times as you like.

Can I change the color of each style?

Yes. Every style card has a color picker with quick swatches and a custom color input. The color copies with the text as rich text: paste into Google Docs, Word or an email and it stays colored. Bio and username fields on Instagram, TikTok and Discord are plain text only, so they show the fancy characters without color.

Why does my fancy text show as boxes (□□□) on some apps?

Some older devices or apps don't support all Unicode characters. Try a simpler style like Bold, Small Caps or Fullwidth, which have the widest support. Bubble and Gothic styles need newer systems.

Can I use it for Instagram, TikTok and Discord?

Absolutely — that's what it's made for. Bios, usernames, captions, comments and server nicknames all work. Just copy here and paste there. Note: some platforms filter a few symbols in usernames, but bios and captions accept almost everything.

Is my text uploaded or stored?

No. Everything happens locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to a server, stored or tracked. See our Privacy Policy for details.
